The toughest gym in Thane

Fitoholic Raw is a raw, no-nonsense studio built around your own bodyweight. Pull-up rigs, rings, parallettes and open mat space — no machines to hide behind. Every rep is coached for form, because right form is what gives you results.

Founder Jayesh Patole is a transformation coach, nutritionist and the author of How To Get In Your Best Shape. New members start with a free movement assessment and a trial class.
